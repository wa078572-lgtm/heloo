1The Problem

A phone has ~200 tracked items (chips, firmware, software, parts, certificates), sold in many variants, and something changes every month. Without control:

  • Nobody knows exactly what is in each variant right now
  • Changes slip in without approval
  • Regions get left behind on old software
  • Proving the configuration to an auditor takes days

2The Solution

For every item and variant, the CCD compares three things:

APPROVEDActive baselinewhat it should be
ACTUALGitHub + PLMwhat is deployed
AUTHORIZEDJira ECRswhat was approved
↓  compare  ↓
✓ Match, or covered by an approved ECR
Compliant: green, traceable to the ECR
● Differs with no approved ECR
Critical “unauthorized change” alarm

3Change Control

Open→ Impact Review→ CCB Review→ Approved→ Promote baseline→ Implemented ↳Rejected: alarm stays
Promote refuses anything unapproved. Each approved baseline is sealed with a SHA-256 fingerprint, so tampering is detected.
